# Montana Filing Guide — Love Rescue, Inc.

Decisions locked in (July 18, 2026): **incorporate in Montana**, as a **fresh nonprofit
corporation** (no LLC exists yet, so the nonprofit can simply *be* Love Rescue — and the
"successor to a for-profit" 1023-EZ disqualifier doesn't apply), **3+ board members are
lined up**, and projected gross receipts are **under $50k/yr → Form 1023-EZ ($275)**.

Montana turns out to be one of the easiest states in the country for this:

| Item | Montana answer |
|---|---|
| Articles of Incorporation fee | **$10** ([SOS fee schedule](https://sosmt.gov/business/fees/)) |
| How filed | **Online only** at [biz.sosmt.gov](https://biz.sosmt.gov) (paper not accepted); create an account via "Login → Create An Account" |
| Annual report | Due **Jan 1 – Apr 15** each year starting the year after incorporation, **$10** ($15 late fee; dissolution by Dec 1 if ignored) |
| Charitable solicitation registration | **Not required in Montana** — one of ~11 states without it ([MT Nonprofit Association](https://mtnonprofit.org/mna-resources/starting-a-501c3-in-montana/)) |
| State sales tax | Montana has none — no exemption paperwork needed |
| Registered agent | Required, with a Montana street address (you or any board member living in MT can serve; commercial agents ~$100–$150/yr if preferred) |

## Filing walkthrough (biz.sosmt.gov)

1. **Create an account** at [biz.sosmt.gov](https://biz.sosmt.gov) (Login → Create An Account).
   Note: the portal's business search sits behind this login too — the name **"Love Rescue"**
   gets checked for conflicts in real time when you type it into the filing, so availability
   resolves itself at this step. Have a backup name ready (e.g., "Love Rescue Foundation").
2. **Register A Business → Domestic Nonprofit Corporation**, and enter:
   - Name: `Love Rescue, Inc.` (or available variant)
   - Type: **Public Benefit Corporation** (Montana asks you to classify as public benefit /
     mutual benefit / religious — public benefit is the one that matches 501(c)(3) charity status)
   - Members: **no members** (board-governed, per the bylaws draft)
   - Registered agent: `[NAME + MONTANA STREET ADDRESS]`
   - Principal office: `[STREET ADDRESS — reused later for SAM.gov, so not a P.O. box]`
   - Directors/incorporator: `[YOUR 3+ BOARD NAMES]`
   - **Purpose / additional provisions:** paste the three clauses (purpose,
     prohibited-activities, dissolution) from `02-ORGANIZING-DOCUMENTS-DRAFTS.md` **verbatim**
     into the optional-provisions/purpose text area. Montana's form doesn't require them —
     **the IRS does.** Filing without them means amending later before the IRS will approve.
3. Pay the **$10** fee. The portal noted (July 2026) it is reviewing applications within
   about two weeks of submission; you'll see status in your Work Queue.
4. When approved, download the stamped articles PDF — the IRS and the bank both want it.

## Then, in order (details in `05-ACTION-ITEMS.md`)

1. First board meeting: adopt bylaws + conflict-of-interest policy, elect officers (drafts ready).
2. **EIN** — free, instant, [IRS online](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-ss-4), needs your SSN as responsible party.
3. **Form 1023-EZ** on Pay.gov — $275; every answer is pre-drafted in `03-FORM-1023-EZ-WORKSHEET.md`;
   with the Montana + fresh-entity + under-$50k decisions, fill in: State = MT,
   incorporation date = `[from stamped articles]`, NTEE = P40. Expect a determination
   letter in ~2–4 weeks.
4. **Montana Dept. of Revenue** — after the IRS letter arrives, file for the state
   corporate income tax exemption (simple form referencing the federal determination).
5. **SAM.gov → Grants.gov** registration, then execute the grant strategy in
   `04-FEDERAL-GRANTS.md`.
