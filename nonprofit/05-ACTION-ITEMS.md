# Action Items That Require You (the Founder/Officer)

Everything researchable and draftable has been done. The remaining steps are legal acts that
must be performed by a human officer: they involve signatures under penalty of perjury, your
SSN, and payments. Each is prepared so it should take minutes.

## 1. Two decisions (reply in chat and drafting can be finalized)

- **State of incorporation** — normally your home state.
- **Board members** — 3+ names; majority unrelated to each other/you is strongly recommended.
- (Also confirm: fresh nonprofit affiliate — recommended — vs. converting the existing
  Love Rescue business. See `01-501c3-ROADMAP.md` Step 1.)

## 2. File Articles of Incorporation (~15 min online, ~$25–$125)

Your Secretary of State's website → nonprofit corporation filing → paste the purpose,
prohibited-activities, and dissolution clauses from `02-ORGANIZING-DOCUMENTS-DRAFTS.md`
verbatim. Ideally have a nonprofit attorney glance at it first.

## 3. Hold the first board meeting (~30 min)

Use the agenda at the bottom of `02-ORGANIZING-DOCUMENTS-DRAFTS.md`: adopt bylaws + COI
policy, elect officers, authorize the EIN/1023 filings and a bank account. Keep signed minutes.

## 4. Get the EIN (~10 min, free, instant)

[IRS online EIN application](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-ss-4) (Mon–Fri
7am–10pm ET). You are the "responsible party" and must enter **your own SSN** — this is why
it can't be delegated to anyone, including an AI agent.

## 5. File the 501(c)(3) application on Pay.gov ($275 or $600)

First make the honest 1023-EZ vs. full-1023 call in `03-FORM-1023-EZ-WORKSHEET.md` (it hinges
on whether you project ≤$50k/yr gross receipts for 3 years). Then create a Pay.gov account,
search "1023-EZ" (or "1023"), and transcribe the pre-drafted answers. You sign under penalty
of perjury and pay from the organization's funds.

## 6. On approval: SAM.gov → Grants.gov (free)

Register the entity at SAM.gov with the EIN (allow ~10 business days; renew annually), get
the UEI, set yourself as EBiz POC, create the Grants.gov account. Then the partnership and
application strategy in `04-FEDERAL-GRANTS.md` §"Recommended application strategy" is ready
to execute — and drafting the actual partnership letters, subaward pitches to current HMRF
grantees, and grant narratives is work that CAN be done for you on request.

---

### Why these weren't done automatically

Submitting federal filings requires attestations only an authorized officer can lawfully
make, an SSN-linked identity, and fee payments — and every downstream step (IRS application,
SAM.gov, grant submissions) is impossible until the state corporation legally exists. Nothing
here is blocked on research or drafting; it's blocked on signatures that must be yours.
