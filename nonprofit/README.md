# Love Rescue Nonprofit Formation & Federal Funding Package

**Mission:** Restore the relationships of people who are underserved in their communities.

This directory contains everything needed to form **Love Rescue** (or a nonprofit affiliate of it)
as a 501(c)(3) public charity and pursue federal grant funding. Research current as of July 2026,
sourced from IRS.gov, Grants.gov, SAM.gov, ACF/HHS, and SAMHSA.

## Why the last six boxes aren't checked

Federal filings (IRS Form 1023-EZ, EIN application, SAM.gov registration, Grants.gov
submissions) must be signed/attested by an authorized officer of the organization under
penalty of perjury, are tied to a real person's SSN, and require payment from the
organization's own funds. No AI agent can lawfully make those attestations on your behalf —
and until the state incorporation exists, the downstream applications are impossible to file
at all. `05-ACTION-ITEMS.md` walks through exactly what to do; each step is prepared so it
takes minutes, not days.
