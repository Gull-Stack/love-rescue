# 501(c)(3) Roadmap for Love Rescue

Path from "nothing" to IRS-recognized public charity. Researched July 2026 from IRS.gov and
related federal sources (links at each step).

---

## Overview of the sequence

```
1. Choose state + name          (1 day, founder decision)
2. Recruit board (3+ people)    (founder decision)
3. File Articles of Incorporation with the state   (~$50–$125, days–weeks)
4. Adopt bylaws + conflict-of-interest policy at first board meeting
5. Get an EIN from the IRS      (free, instant online)
6. File Form 1023-EZ on Pay.gov ($275, ~2–4 weeks to determination)
7. State charitable registration + ongoing compliance
8. SAM.gov → Grants.gov registration → grant applications
```

Total hard costs: roughly **$325–$400** (state filing fee + $275 IRS fee).
Realistic total timeline: **4–10 weeks** from filing articles to IRS determination letter.

---

## Step 1 — Organize under state law (prerequisite to everything)

The IRS requires that an applicant already be **organized as a corporation, trust, or
unincorporated association** under state law before applying for exemption
([IRS: Application process](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/application-process)).
For a grant-seeking charity, a **nonprofit corporation** is the standard choice — grantmakers
and SAM.gov expect it.

- File **Articles of Incorporation** with the Secretary of State in your home state
  (typically $25–$125; most states have online filing with same-day or few-day turnaround).
- The articles **must** contain an IRS-compliant **purpose clause** and **dissolution clause**
  or the 501(c)(3) application will fail the *organizational test*. Exact drafted language is
  in `02-ORGANIZING-DOCUMENTS-DRAFTS.md`.
- Name a **registered agent** with a street address in the state.
- **Note on the existing Love Rescue business:** if Love Rescue currently operates as a
  for-profit (the app, billing, App Store presence), the cleanest structure is a **new
  nonprofit corporation** (e.g., "Love Rescue Foundation") rather than converting the
  existing entity. A nonprofit cannot have owners or distribute profit, and converting a
  revenue-generating app company has tax consequences. Talk to a nonprofit attorney or CPA
  before choosing conversion over a fresh entity.

## Step 2 — Board of directors

- Most states require at least **1–3 directors**; the IRS strongly prefers a board of
  **3+ members, a majority unrelated** by family or business. A one-person or all-family
  board is a common trigger for IRS follow-up questions and grantmaker rejection.
- No earnings may benefit insiders (**private inurement** is prohibited), and the org may not
  substantially lobby or participate in political campaigns at all
  ([IRS: Exemption requirements](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/exemption-requirements-501c3-organizations)).

## Step 3 — Bylaws and conflict-of-interest policy

Adopt at the first board meeting; keep signed minutes. Drafts are in
`02-ORGANIZING-DOCUMENTS-DRAFTS.md`. Neither is filed with the state, but the IRS and every
grantmaker will ask for them.

## Step 4 — EIN (Employer Identification Number)

- Free, online at IRS.gov, issued **immediately** (tool open Mon–Fri 7am–10pm ET)
  ([IRS: About Form SS-4](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-ss-4)).
- Must be requested **after** the state incorporation exists, using the exact legal name.
- Requires a **responsible party**: an individual (not an entity) who controls the org —
  generally the principal officer — identified by **name and SSN/ITIN**. Nominees are not
  allowed ([IRS: Responsible parties](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/responsible-parties-and-nominees)).
  This is why the EIN step cannot be done by anyone but the founding officer.

## Step 5 — Apply for 501(c)(3) recognition

Two routes ([IRS user-fee page](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-1023-and-1023-ez-amount-of-user-fee)):

| | **Form 1023-EZ** | **Form 1023 (full)** |
|---|---|---|
| User fee (non-refundable) | **$275** | **$600** |
| Filed via | Pay.gov (online only) | Pay.gov (online only) |
| Typical processing | **2–4 weeks** (automated) | **3–6 months** |
| Eligibility | Projected gross receipts **≤ $50,000/yr** for the next 3 years AND total assets **≤ $250,000**; must pass the 30-question Eligibility Worksheet in the [1023-EZ instructions](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1023-ez) | Everyone else (schools, hospitals, churches, larger orgs) |

**Recommendation for Love Rescue:** start with **Form 1023-EZ** if you genuinely project
≤$50k/yr in gross receipts for the first 3 years. If grant plans mean you expect more than
$50k/yr (one SAMHSA award alone exceeds this), file the **full Form 1023** instead — filing
1023-EZ while projecting larger receipts is a misrepresentation. The pre-drafted worksheet in
`03-FORM-1023-EZ-WORKSHEET.md` covers both scenarios.

- The applicant signs **under penalty of perjury**. Only an authorized officer/director may sign.
- When approved, the determination letter is retroactive to incorporation if filed within
  27 months of formation.

## Step 6 — Post-approval compliance (don't skip)

- **Annual IRS filing:** Form 990-N (e-Postcard, gross receipts ≤ $50k), 990-EZ, or 990.
  **Missing 3 consecutive years = automatic revocation.**
- **State charitable solicitation registration:** ~40 states require registering *before*
  fundraising from their residents (relevant since the app is nationwide).
- **State tax exemptions** (income, sales, property) are separate state applications.
- Keep board minutes, follow the conflict-of-interest policy, arm's-length contracts with any
  related for-profit (critical if the nonprofit licenses or uses the Love Rescue app).

## Step 7 — Federal funding readiness

Covered in detail in `04-FEDERAL-GRANTS.md`. Short version: **SAM.gov entity registration**
(free, needs EIN, allow up to 10 business days, renew annually) → get the **Unique Entity ID
(UEI)** → designate an **EBiz POC** → register on **Grants.gov** → apply
([Grants.gov: Applicant registration](https://www.grants.gov/applicants/applicant-registration)).
