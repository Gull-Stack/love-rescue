## A. Subaward pitch letter — to current ACF HMRF grantees (usable TODAY)

> Subject: Curriculum + technology partner for your HMRF program serving underserved families
>
> Dear `[PROGRAM DIRECTOR]`,
>
> I lead Love Rescue, a Montana-based relationship-restoration organization (501(c)(3)
> application in progress) built around a structured 16-week relationship curriculum
> delivered through a mobile platform with built-in engagement analytics and outcome
> tracking. Our mission is restoring the relationships of people who are underserved in
> their communities.
>
> I'm writing because your `[HEART / FORGE / READY4Life]` grant serves exactly the
> population we built for, and ACF's performance-measurement expectations are exactly what
> our platform automates: enrollment, session completion, pre/post relationship-quality
> measures, and longitudinal follow-up — reported per participant, exportable for nFORM.
>
> Concretely, we can offer your program: (1) a digital delivery channel for participants
> who can't attend in person, (2) automated data collection for your federal reporting,
> and (3) our curriculum as supplementary content. We're seeking a modest subaward or
> fee-for-service arrangement scoped to your needs.
>
> Could we schedule 30 minutes in the next two weeks?
>
> `[NAME, TITLE, CONTACT]`

Find target grantees on the [HMRF grantee map](https://acf.gov/ofa/map/healthy-marriage-responsible-fatherhood-grantee-locations)
— start with grantees in Montana and neighboring states, then the largest HEART grantees.

## C. Federal narrative boilerplate (reusable across SRAE/PREP/SAMHSA-partner applications)

**Need statement (localize with data):**
> `[COUNTY/REGION]` families face compounding barriers to relationship stability: `[POVERTY
> RATE]`% live below the federal poverty line, the nearest family-counseling provider is
> `[N]` miles away, and `[STATE/COUNTY]` divorce and single-parent-household rates exceed
> national averages. Relationship distress is a documented driver of poorer behavioral
> health, child outcomes, and economic instability — yet relationship education in
> `[REGION]` is effectively unavailable to those who cannot pay privately.
> (Pull local figures from census.gov QuickFacts and county health rankings.)

**Approach:**
> Love Rescue delivers a 16-week, skills-based relationship education program through a
> hybrid model: facilitated group sessions hosted by community partners, reinforced daily
> through a mobile platform that provides exercises, assessments, and progress tracking.
> The digital layer removes the two barriers that most often exclude underserved
> participants — transportation and scheduling — and gives funders per-participant,
> per-session outcome data rather than attendance counts alone.

**Organizational capacity (honest new-org framing):**
> Love Rescue is a newly formed organization built on a mature, production technology
> platform. Its capacity strategy pairs experienced community partners (`[NAMED PARTNER
> ORGS]`) for local delivery with Love Rescue's measurement infrastructure — and its board
> includes `[RELEVANT EXPERIENCE]`.

**Outcomes & evaluation:**
> Primary measures: relationship satisfaction (pre/mid/post standardized scale),
> program completion rate, and 6-month retention of gains. All measures are collected
> in-app, timestamped, and reportable by cohort and demographic segment.
