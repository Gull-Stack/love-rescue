# Form 1023-EZ — Pre-Drafted Answers

Form 1023-EZ is filed **online only at Pay.gov** with a **$275 non-refundable fee**, signed by
an officer **under penalty of perjury**. Typical processing: 2–4 weeks.
([IRS: About Form 1023-EZ](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1023-ez))

## ⚠️ Eligibility gate — answer this honestly first

You may use 1023-EZ **only if** projected **gross receipts ≤ $50,000 in each of the next 3
years** (and past 3, if any) **and total assets ≤ $250,000**, and you pass the full
Eligibility Worksheet in the instructions (~30 questions; disqualifiers include churches,
schools, hospitals, foreign orgs, LLCs, successors to for-profits).

> **Decision point for Love Rescue:** a single federal grant (SAMHSA awards run to $1M/yr)
> blows past $50k. If the funding plan in `04-FEDERAL-GRANTS.md` is realistic for years 1–3,
> file the **full Form 1023 ($600, 3–6 months)** instead. If year 1–3 will genuinely be small
> (donations + small foundation grants while building capacity), 1023-EZ is fine and faster,
> and you can grow past $50k later without penalty as long as the projection was honest when made.
> **"Successor to a for-profit entity" is a 1023-EZ disqualifier** — if the nonprofit takes
> over the existing Love Rescue app business (rather than being a fresh affiliate), you must
> use the full Form 1023.

## Part II — Organizational Structure

- Type: **Corporation**
- Incorporation date / state: `[DATE / STATE]`
- Attests that articles contain the required **purpose** and **dissolution** clauses →
  they will, if filed from `02-ORGANIZING-DOCUMENTS-DRAFTS.md`. Check both boxes truthfully
  only after the state accepts the articles.

## Part III — Specific Activities

- **NTEE code:** `P40` (Family Services) — alternatives: `F22`/`F30`-series (counseling,
  mental health treatment) if programming becomes clinical.
- **Mission statement (250-char limit), draft:**
  > Provides free and low-cost relationship education, skills training, and restorative
  > support to underserved individuals, couples, and families to strengthen relationships
  > and family stability.
- Attest: no campaign intervention; no substantial lobbying; no private benefit — **yes** (and
  make it true operationally).
- Compensation to officers/directors: answer truthfully; "no" at formation is typical.

## Part IV — Foundation Classification

- Elect **public charity** status under **509(a)(2)** (gross-receipts test — fits a
  program-fee + donations model) or **509(a)(1)/170(b)(1)(A)(vi)** (publicly supported —
  fits a donations/grants model). For a grant-funded plan, **509(a)(1)** is the usual choice.

## If filing the full Form 1023 instead

Everything above still applies, plus you'll need: a **narrative description of activities**
(the 16-week curriculum in `CURRICULUM-16-WEEKS.md` plus coach/therapist programming is the
backbone — describe who is served, how, where, by whom, and how it's funded), **3–4 years of
budget projections**, copies of articles/bylaws/COI policy, and compensation details. Fee
**$600**, processing 3–6 months.
